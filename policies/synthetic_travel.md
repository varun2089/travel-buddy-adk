# Synthetic training policy — not Monash policy
Document-ID: SYN-TRAVEL
Version: v1

## REVIEW-01 | Human review
This fictional training policy requires a human to review travel proposals. A saved proposal is a draft awaiting review. A proposed budget is not an approved budget. Policy search does not approve travel, authorise purchases or submit proposals.

## EXP-01 | Expense evidence
For this fictional training exercise, retain itemised receipts for accommodation and local transport. If evidence is missing, record the gap for the human reviewer rather than claiming reimbursement eligibility.

## EXP-02 | Registration and unknown costs
Record conference registration separately from flights, accommodation and local transport. An unknown fee is unknown, not zero. A trip with unknown costs has no confirmed complete total. Do not adjust estimates to fit a proposed budget.

## SCOPE-01 | Scope and unavailable evidence
These synthetic examples are not Monash policies, official university advice or live rates. They specify no real reimbursement caps, visa rules or insurance coverage. Consult the responsible human for matters not supported by retrieved evidence. This prototype performs no automatic bookings or real submissions.
